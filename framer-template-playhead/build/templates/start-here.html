Start here · Playhead template guide

Make it yours in an afternoon.

This page ships inside the template (unpublished, so visitors never see it). It walks you through the customization order that works best, plus copy-paste prompts for the Framer Agent.

1. Order of work

  1. Site Settings → General: site name, favicon, social image (assets folder has an OG cover you can adapt).
  2. Colors & text styles: swap the six color styles first; everything updates.
  3. CMS → Projects: replace the 12 demo projects with your own (video URL + poster + client + result).
  4. CMS → Testimonials: three to six quotes. Initials avatars are automatic.
  5. Home: hero copy, stats, packages, FAQ. Then Services, About, Contact.
  6. Contact: connect the form (Framer Forms) and paste your Cal.com / Calendly embed.
  7. Publish on a custom domain and run the Framer performance check.

2. Colors & fonts

Six color styles drive the whole template: Paper, Paper 2, Ink, Screen, On Screen, Accent. Change Accent (default signal orange #FF4D1A) to your brand color and keep the paper/ink contrast for readability.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels & timecodes). All three are in Framer’s font library — pick them from the text-style panel, no upload needed.

3. CMS collections

Playhead uses exactly two collections, so it runs on the Framer Basic plan.

Projects

  • Title (title) · Slug · Client (text) · Format (option: Short-form, Long-form, Ads, Motion, Podcast)
  • Platform (option: YouTube, TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Web) · Orientation (option: 16:9, 9:16)
  • Poster (image) · Video URL (link: YouTube/Vimeo/MP4) · Result (text, e.g. “2.4M views”)
  • Summary (text) · Brief, Approach, Outcome (formatted text) · Metrics 1–3 (text) · Stills (gallery) · Featured (toggle) · Date

Testimonials

  • Quote (text) · Name · Role · Initials · Color (color) · Featured (toggle)

4. Adding video

  • Showreel: replace the poster in the Showreel component with a Framer Video layer (upload MP4, muted autoplay off) or a YouTube/Vimeo embed.
  • Reels wall: each Reel card has a Poster image and a Video layer set to play on hover. Use 9:16 MP4s under 8 MB for smooth hover-play.
  • Project pages: the Video URL field feeds an embed component; posters keep the page fast until play.

5. Pages

Home · Work · Project (CMS detail) · Services · About · Contact · 404 · Legal · Start here (this page). Delete what you don’t need; the nav and footer are components, so links update everywhere.

6. SEO & publish

  • Every page has a title and description field pre-filled — rewrite them with your name and city (“Video editor in Berlin”).
  • Alt text is set on all demo images; keep the habit for your posters.
  • Run Site Settings → Performance before publishing; keep videos under 8 MB or embed them.
