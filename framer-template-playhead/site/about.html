About

Eight years in the timeline.

I started cutting skate videos on a borrowed laptop. Today I edit for creators with millions of subscribers and brands that want their films to feel like creator content. The tools changed; the job didn’t: make people feel something before they can scroll.

Berlin Remote worldwide

How I work

Story first, then pace, then polish. I read the retention graph of every video I ship and I will tell you when a hook isn’t working — even if it was your favorite line. Most clients stay for years because the second video is always better than the first.

I work remote from Berlin, on European and US hours, with a two-person crew for motion-heavy projects.
